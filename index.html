Ledger Relay

iMessage companion for Ledger

A tiny menu bar app that connects your iMessages to Ledger on your iPhone. It reads incoming messages, scores them, and lets you reply — all from the Ledger app.

Download for Mac

Setup

1

Unzip and drag Ledger Relay to your Applications folder.

2

Open it. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

3

Enter the pairing code shown in the Ledger app on your iPhone.

4

Grant Full Disk Access when prompted — Ledger Relay needs this to read your iMessages.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. Runs silently in your menu bar and launches at login. Your Mac needs to be on for syncing.
