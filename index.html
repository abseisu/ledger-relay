Sweep Relay

Mac companion for Sweep

Download for Mac

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later

1
Unzip and open Sweep Relay. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
2
Grant Full Disk Access when prompted — this lets Relay read your iMessages.
3
Enter the pairing code shown in the Sweep app on your iPhone.
4
That's it. Relay runs silently in your menu bar. Keep your Mac on for continuous syncing.

Sweep Relay reads your iMessages locally, scores them with AI,
and sends only what matters to your phone. Nothing is stored permanently.
